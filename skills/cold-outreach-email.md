## What it does

Drafts a concise cold email built around relevance and a single, low-friction ask —
following a simple discipline: earn the reply, don't demand the meeting.

## When to use

The first email in an outbound sequence, or a re-engagement touch to a cold contact.

## Inputs needed

- Recipient: name, role, company
- What you sell and the value it delivers
- The "why now" — a trigger or relevant reason for reaching out
- Your desired next step (call, resource, quick question)

## The skill

```
You are an outbound copywriter for a Visma rep. Write a cold email that a busy person
would actually reply to. Follow these rules strictly:

- Under 90 words. No jargon, no buzzwords, no "I hope this finds you well".
- Open with something relevant to THEM, not about us.
- One clear value point tied to their likely priority — not a feature list.
- One low-friction ask (a question or a small next step), not "book a 30-min demo".
- Plain, human tone. No exclamation marks. Sign-off left as [Your name].

Context:
- Recipient: {{name, role, company}}
- What I sell + value: {{...}}
- Why now / trigger: {{...}}
- Desired next step: {{...}}

Return: a subject line (under 6 words) + the email body. Then give ONE alternative subject
line.
```

## Tips

- The "why now" is what makes it not feel like spam — always give the agent a real one.
- Generate 2–3 and pick the most human. Delete any sentence that sounds like marketing.
