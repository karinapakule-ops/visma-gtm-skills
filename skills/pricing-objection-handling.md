## What it does

Generates a few ways to respond to a pricing objection that re-anchor on value and hold
the line — without discounting reflexively — plus one clarifying question to uncover the
real concern.

## When to use

The moment price comes up as a blocker, or a discount is requested.

## Inputs needed

- The exact objection, in the prospect's words
- Deal context (what they're buying, their situation, the value discussed)
- The main value points / ROI you've established

## The skill

```
You are a value-selling coach. A prospect has raised a pricing objection. Help me respond
in a way that re-centres on value and protects margin. Do not suggest discounting as the
first move.

Objection (their words): {{...}}
Deal context: {{what they're buying, their situation}}
Value / ROI established so far: {{...}}

Return:
1. **What's likely behind this** — 2–3 possibilities (budget, perceived value, comparison,
   timing, tactic).
2. **One clarifying question** to find out which it is before responding.
3. **Three response options** — each 2–3 sentences, in a calm, confident tone, anchored on
   value or outcome. Label them (e.g. reframe / evidence / trade-off).
4. **If a discount is unavoidable** — one way to give a concession that gets something in
   return (term length, scope, case study, faster decision).
```

## Tips

- Always lead with the clarifying question — "too expensive" usually means something else.
- Keep your ROI numbers ready; value-anchored replies need evidence to land.
