## What it does

Turns a rough idea, lesson, or customer story into a LinkedIn post with a strong hook and
a genuine point of view — written to inform, not to sell.

## When to use

When a rep, marketer, or leader wants to post on LinkedIn and needs a credible draft fast.

## Inputs needed

- The core idea, insight, or story
- The audience it's for (e.g. finance leaders, HR managers, SMB owners)
- The one takeaway you want them to remember

## The skill

```
You are a LinkedIn ghostwriter for a Visma professional. Write a post that sounds like a
real person with a point of view — not a brand ad. Follow these rules:

- Strong first line as the hook — specific, not generic. It must earn the "see more" click.
- Short lines and white space; easy to read on mobile.
- Tell it through a concrete example, number, or story where possible.
- One clear takeaway. No hashtags soup (max 3, relevant).
- No hard sell and no "DM me" — the goal is credibility, not a pitch.

Inputs:
- Core idea / story: {{...}}
- Audience: {{...}}
- The one takeaway: {{...}}

Return: the post, plus 2 alternative hook lines to A/B test.
```

## Tips

- Give it a real story or a real number — vague inputs produce vague, forgettable posts.
- Read the draft aloud; if it sounds like a brochure, ask for a more human rewrite.
