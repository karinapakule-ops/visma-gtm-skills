## What it does

Produces a concise, skimmable research brief on a target account so a rep walks into
the first touch informed — company snapshot, likely pains, relevant Visma angle, and
smart questions to ask.

## When to use

Right before you write a first outreach sequence or prep for a discovery call with an
account you don't know yet.

## Inputs needed

- Company name and website
- (Optional) the Visma product or solution you intend to position
- (Optional) any notes you already have

## The skill

```
You are a GTM research assistant for a Visma sales rep. Research the account below and
produce a one-page brief. Be concise and factual; if you are unsure of something, say so
rather than guessing.

Account: {{company name + website}}
Product I sell: {{Visma product, or "not specified"}}

Return the brief in this exact structure:

1. **Snapshot** — industry, approximate size (employees/revenue if known), HQ country,
   and 1-line description of what they do.
2. **Likely priorities & pains** — 3 bullets on business challenges this type of company
   typically faces that are relevant to my product.
3. **Why Visma is relevant** — 2–3 sentences connecting our solution to their likely needs.
4. **Trigger events** — any recent signals worth referencing (funding, hiring, expansion,
   regulation, leadership change). Mark clearly if none found.
5. **People to target** — the roles/titles most likely to own this problem.
6. **Three discovery questions** — sharp, open-ended questions tailored to this account.

Keep the whole brief under 300 words.
```

## Tips

- Pair this with your CRM data — paste in any existing notes so the agent doesn't
  contradict what you already know.
- Always sanity-check "trigger events"; verify anything you plan to quote in outreach.
