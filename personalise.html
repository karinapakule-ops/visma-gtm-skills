Before you rely on a skill

Personalise a skill first

These skills are validated templates, not plug-and-play. Tailor them to your company, your portal, and your voice first — a little setup is what makes the output trustworthy, not generic AI.

⚙️ The five things to set up

  • Start with Company Context. Fill in the Company Context template with your product, ICP, buyer personas, and value proposition. Every content-generating skill (emails, briefs, prep) reads from it, so outputs sound like your business — not generic AI.
  • Add your voice. Layer a personal voice/style skill so drafted emails sound like you. Tone mismatch is the fastest way to kill adoption.
  • Swap the framework if needed. Using MEDDPICC or BANT instead of SPICED? Open the skill's SKILL.md and adjust the framework section — the structure stays the same.
  • Match your HubSpot portal. Deploy HubSpot API Best Practices first, and confirm the properties and deal stages a skill writes to actually exist in your portal.
  • Validate before production. Test each skill in your own HubSpot sandbox, and pilot the write-back skills with one or two reps for a couple of weeks before rolling out widely.

Recommended order: start with the read-only skills (Account Research Brief, Daily Action Digest) → deploy the Foundations skills → then pilot the write-back skills.
